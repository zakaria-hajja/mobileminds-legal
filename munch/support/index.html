Munch Support

Help for Munch: Subscription Creatures, on Android and iPhone

Contact us

Write to mblminds@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, its system version and Munch's version (shown in the app under Settings). Please don't send the names or prices of your subscriptions: we don't need them to help. You can also open the email from inside the app with Settings > Contact support.

What Munch is

Munch keeps track of the subscriptions you pay for. Each one is a little creature that "eats" on its renewal day. Munch shows what your subscriptions cost per month or per year and reminds you before a renewal or before a free trial ends. On the renewal day you feed a creature if you're keeping the subscription, or release it once you have cancelled. Doing that earns points, which you can spend on time without ads.

Munch is free. It has no account and no login, and it doesn't connect to your bank, cards or email.

Questions and answers

Where is my data stored? Do you see my subscriptions?

Your subscriptions (names, prices, dates, reminder settings) stay on your phone. Munch has no server of its own and never sends them to us. Munch uses Google services for its ad, for anonymous usage counts and for crash reports; none of them receives your subscriptions. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is my data backed up? What happens on a new phone?

Munch has no cloud backup of its own. Your phone's own backup can include Munch's data, and how it comes back depends on your phone:

  • Android: with backup to your Google account on, your creatures come back when you set up a new phone from that backup, or when you reinstall Munch while signed in to the same Google account. If backup is off, uninstalling Munch removes its data for good.
  • iPhone: an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone includes Munch's data, but it comes back only when you restore the whole iPhone (or set up a new iPhone) from that backup. Deleting Munch deletes its data; reinstalling it from the App Store starts empty, even with iCloud Backup on.

How do I delete my data?

  • One subscription: open the creature. If you added it by mistake, choose Delete (added by mistake). A released creature can be removed with Remove from Released.
  • Everything: uninstall Munch. On Android you can also go to Settings > Apps > Munch > Storage > Clear storage. A phone backup may still hold a copy until that backup is replaced or deleted.
  • For the data the Google services receive, see section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

My reminders don't arrive

Reminders are notifications that Munch schedules on your phone. Check these in order:

  • Notification permission. If Munch shows "Reminders are off" or "Reminders are blocked" on Home or in Settings, tap the button there. If you said no earlier, turn notifications on in your phone's settings: on Android, Settings > Apps > Munch > Notifications; on iPhone, Settings > Notifications > Munch.
  • The creature's reminder. Open the creature and check that its next renewal date is right. Reminder Off means you only get the reminder on the renewal day; pick a number of days to also be reminded before it.
  • Reminder time. Munch reminds you at the time set in Settings > Reminder time.
  • Battery saving, Focus and Do Not Disturb can hold back or hide notifications. Some Android phones also stop apps in the background; allowing Munch to run unrestricted in its battery settings helps.
  • Open Munch from time to time: each time it opens, it plans the next reminders again.

Reminders help but can be late or missed, so please also keep an eye on your renewal dates.

Why are there ads? How do I get ad-free time?

Munch is free and shows one small banner ad, on the Home screen only. You earn points by feeding creatures on their renewal day, releasing creatures you have cancelled, catching a free trial before it turns into a paid subscription, and unlocking badges. On the Rewards screen you can spend points on ad-free time: the banner goes away right away and comes back when the time is up. Points can't be bought and have no money value.

Where the law requires it, Munch asks for your ad and analytics choices on first launch. You can change them later in Settings > Privacy choices when that row is shown. On iPhone, you can turn tracking off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

I released a creature. Is my subscription cancelled?

No. Munch only tracks your subscriptions and can't cancel them for you. To stop paying, cancel with the provider you pay:

  • Paid through Google Play: Play Store app > your profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Paid through Apple: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
  • Paid on the service's website (or by card or bank): sign in to your account with that service and cancel there, or contact the service.

Then release the creature in Munch so it stops counting in your totals.

Can I undo a release or a feed?

Yes. Right after feeding or releasing, tap Undo. Later, open a released creature and choose Restore to make it active again.
